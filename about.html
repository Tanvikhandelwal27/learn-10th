About this learning space

Clear explanations, original practice, and a calm place to make sense of history.

Built around understanding

This independent learning resource organises concise explanations, people, dates, terms, timelines, and original practice around the five chapters in the Class 10 NCERT History syllabus. It is designed to help learners connect ideas, not memorise a replacement textbook.

One home for four subjects

Subjects and chapter content live in structured JavaScript data. History is available now. Geography, Political Science / Civics, and Economics are visible as future modules and can be populated without rebuilding the shared navigation, search, filters, or page layouts.

Use your textbook, too

These notes support study and revision; they are not a substitute for the prescribed textbook or your teacher's guidance. Learning explanations and questions here are original concise summaries and practice prompts.
